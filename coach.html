📏 How to Measure
🎳
Standard Method
Deliver a bowl with normal draw weight. Time from release until it stops. Average 3 readings. That is your green speed in seconds.
Coaching TipGreen speed changes during the day. Measure before sessions and note conditions to build a picture over the season.
📊 Speed Scale

Higher seconds = faster green. The bowl rolls further for the same delivery effort.

Slow
🇬🇧 UK early season, wet

🌿 Factors Affecting Speed
✂️
Grass Length
The biggest factor. 1mm difference can change speed by several seconds.
Shorter = Faster
💧
Moisture
Wet greens are significantly slower. Greens speed up as they dry during the day.
Wet = Slower
☀️
Temperature
Hot dry conditions firm the surface. Why Australian greens are naturally faster.
Hot and Dry = Faster
🔄
Rolling
Regular rolling compacts the surface and increases speed.
Regular Rolling = Faster
🎯 The Key Principle

Faster green = less weight + wider line
Slower green = more weight + narrower line

Bias effect increases significantly on faster greens.

🐢 Slow Green (9-12 seconds)
💪
More WeightFuller arm swing and positive step-through.
➡️
Narrow the LineLess bias effect. Most players do not narrow enough.
🎳
Bowl ChoiceWider bias — XP, Legacy SL, Tiger EVO, Sonic.
⚡ Fast Green (14-17 seconds)
🪶
Less WeightShorten backswing. Small errors are amplified.
↗️
Wider LineBias increases dramatically. Most players do not go wide enough.
🎳
Bowl ChoiceNarrower bias — d-tec, Vector VS, Tiger TX, Quantum.
🏠 Indoor vs Outdoor
📉
Going Outdoor After IndoorSignificant slow-down. Be patient and let the green show what it needs.
📈
Going Indoor After OutdoorLightning fast. Widen line dramatically and soften delivery. Allow 4-6 ends to calibrate.
📚 Drills Library
World Bowls endorsed coaching drills — beginner to advanced
🌱 Basic
📈 Progressive
🎮 Games
⚡ Circuit
How to UseRun these in coaching sessions. Vary mat position — short, medium and long. Record results for tracking progress.
🎯 Skill 1 — Jack Delivery and First Bowl Effect
Setup
Player delivers jack to target mark then delivers bowl to finish as close as possible to the jack.
💡 Coach Focus
Assess pre-shot routine, mat approach, delivery action and follow through. Check first bowl effectiveness.
🎯 Skill 2 — Shot Recovery
Setup
Place an opposition bowl close to the jack. Player must draw to beat it — finish closer to the jack.
💡 Coach Focus
Does the player assess the head before delivering? Correct hand? Is weight control sufficient to just beat the shot?
🎯 Skill 3 — Draw Off Line
Setup
Place jack close to rink boundary at 25m. Player draws two bowls forehand and two backhand to the jack.
💡 Coach Focus
Can the player adjust their line for an off-centre jack? This drill exposes hand weakness quickly.
🎯 Skill 4 — Grouping No Jack
Setup
No jack. Player bowls first bowl as the target. Next three bowls delivered to group as close as possible.
💡 Coach Focus
Removes mental pressure of a jack. Reveals line and length consistency issues clearly.
🎯 Skill 5 — Draw to the Ditch
Setup
Place jack on 2m mark. All four bowls must finish between the jack and the ditch.
💡 Coach Focus
Excellent weight control drill. Brilliant for skips who need consistent back bowl placement.
🎳 Warm Up — Delivery Line
Setup
Place two discs along the delivery line. Player bowls over or between the discs. Outcome is NOT the priority.
💡 Coach Focus
Assess pre-shot routine and delivery action. One of the few drills where the result does not matter.
📈 Progressive Drawing Drill

3 players per rink, 2 bowls each. Closest bowl moves up one rink. Furthest moves down. Run for 15 ends.

💡 Coach Value
Identifies consistently accurate players. Exposes hand weaknesses and creates healthy competitive pressure.
📈 40 Bowl Skills Assessment

10 ends, 4 bowls per end. Score each bowl: E=Effective S=Short L=Long W=Wide N=Narrow.

💡 Coach Value
Comprehensive snapshot of a player all-round game. Run at regular intervals to track improvement.
📈 Short Bowl Penalty Game

Normal game. Any bowl finishing more than 30cm short of the jack is removed immediately.

💡 Coach Value
Quickly eliminates the habit of consistently bowling short. Players rapidly improve length when there are consequences.
📈 Line and Speed Adjustment

Player draws to target X. Coach repositions the target. Player must adjust with their next bowl.

💡 Coach Value
Tests the most critical in-game skill — the ability to adjust after seeing where your bowl finishes.
🎮 Rotating Positions

Fours game. After 4 ends all players rotate position. Then 3 bowls, then 2, then 1.

💡 Coach Value
Builds empathy across positions and understanding of what each role needs.
🎮 Phantom Bowler

Coach places opposition bowls on the head to create specific tactical situations. Team plays normally.

💡 Coach Value
The most powerful tactical coaching tool available. Ask questions to develop player thinking.
🎮 Super Challenge

Two teams. Each player plays ONE bowl over TWO ends. Closest counting bowls win the set.

💡 Coach Value
High pressure single bowl situations. Excellent for developing composure under competitive pressure.
⚡ 8-Station Circuit

World Bowls endorsed. Players keep individual scores and move between stations. 8 rinks required.

Station 1 — Jack Delivery Accuracy
8 jacks. Deliver 2 to each of 4 markers at different lengths. Score jacks landing within the target zone.
Station 2 — Ditch Control
Score only jacks finishing between the ditch and the 2m mark.
Station 3 — Line Taking
Bowl between markers. Gradually narrow until bowling only over a discus.
Station 4 — Weight and Centre Return
2m mat, forehand and backhand. Score bowls returning to within 30cm either side of centre.
Station 5 — 2m Zone Play
Cones 1m either side of jack. All bowls must finish between cone and jack.
Station 6 — Playing Around a Bowl
Draw around a wing bowl to jack. Draw between jack and bowl 2m behind. Repeat
