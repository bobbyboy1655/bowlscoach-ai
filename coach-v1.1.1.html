📏 How to Measure
🎳
Standard Method
Deliver a bowl with normal draw weight. Time from release until it stops. Average 3 measurements. This is your green speed in seconds.
💡 Good to Know Green speed changes during the day. A morning green may be slower than after an afternoon of sun. Always roll trial ends before important matches.
📊 The Speed Scale

Higher seconds = faster green. Counterintuitive but that's how it works!

9–12s
Slow
🇬🇧 UK early season, wet conditions
12–14s
Medium
🇬🇧 UK summer, good conditions
14–17s
Fast
🏠 Indoor, 🇦🇺 Australia outdoor
17–20s
Very Fast
🇦🇺 Premium Australian greens
🌍 Regional Context UK outdoor greens: 9–14s. Indoor carpets: 16–18s. Australia outdoor: 14–17s regularly. This is why switching between surfaces is such a challenge.
🌿 What Affects Green Speed?
✂️
Grass Length
The biggest factor. Shorter grass = less friction = faster. A difference of 1mm can change speed by several seconds.
Short = Faster
💧
Moisture
Wet greens are significantly slower. Morning dew, rain or watering all slow the green. Greens speed up as they dry.
Wet = Slower
☀️
Temperature & Sun
Hot dry conditions firm up the surface and speed it up. Why Australian greens are naturally faster.
Hot & Dry = Faster
🔄
Rolling & Maintenance
Regular rolling compacts the surface and increases speed. Good drainage always helps.
Regular Rolling = Faster
🎯 The Key Principle

Faster green → less weight + wider line

Slower green → more weight + narrower line

The bias effect increases on faster greens — your aiming line changes significantly between surfaces.

```
🐢 Slow Green (9–12 seconds)
💪
Increase WeightMore pace needed. Fuller arm swing, positive step-through delivery.
➡️
Narrow Your LineLess bias effect. Aim closer to centre than on faster surfaces.
🎳
Bowl ChoiceWider-biased bowl — e.g. Drakes Pride XP, Taylor Legacy SL, Henselite Tiger.
⚡ Fast Green (14–17 seconds)
🪶
Use Less WeightThe green does the work. Shorten backswing and deliver gently.
↗️
Widen Your LineBias effect increases significantly. Aim much wider than feels natural.
🎳
Bowl ChoiceNarrower-biased bowl — e.g. d-tec, Taylor Vector VS, Henselite Tiger TX.
🏠 Indoor ↔ Outdoor Switch
📉
Going Outdoor After IndoorExpect significantly slower. Many players overbowl badly. Let the green tell you what it needs.
📈
Going Indoor After OutdoorThe carpet will feel lightning fast. Widen your line dramatically and soften your delivery.
⏱️
Allow Adjustment TimeMost players need 4–6 ends to fully calibrate when switching surfaces. Don't panic early.
```
